How quota accounting works

Reserved atomically

Concurrent requests cannot overspend. Units are reserved inside a transaction before jobs are dispatched.

Retries are free

A retry reuses the original reservation. Failed attempts never consume quota twice.

Daily reset

Allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. Unused units do not roll over.
